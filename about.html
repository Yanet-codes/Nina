Who We Are



Da-Ya is an independent Planning, Engineering and Development Consultancy who provide expert advice on all aspects of Urban Development. Da-Ya Consulting Planners and Engineers plc is an Addis Ababa based professional firm founded in 2010.
DaYa was set up by professional Planners and Engineers; founders together have over twenty-five years experience in planning consultancy.

Diverse Sectors across Ethiopia

Da-Ya work with National Governments, Local Governments, Municipalities, developers, national and multi-national companies, house builders, institutions and private individuals. We work across Ethiopia in a wide range of sectors including; Master/Structure Plan, Local Development Plan, Asset Management Plan, Land Inventory and Cadastre System Establishment, Road and Bridge Design, Environmental Impact Assessment.
Our blend of extensive planning and engineering knowledge, market awareness and skills in community engagement set us apart from the competition.

With Experienced, Independent and Impartial Project Managers

We can help to assemble wider consultant teams that are all experts in their areas. We brief the team to ensure that they fully understand the client’s objectives and the wider strategy and we work with them to overcome any hurdles.
As an independent Planning and Development Consultancy we aim to provide a fresh perspective. We are not burdened by cross selling of services and so we can help clients to assemble the best project teams and focus on the specific requirements to secure their goals.
