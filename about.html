Who We Are

Da-Ya is an independent Planning, Engineering and Development Consultancy who provide expert advice on all aspects of Urban Development. Da-Ya Consulting Planners and Engineers plc is an Addis Ababa based professional firm founded in 2010. DaYa was set up by professional Planners and Engineers; founders together have over twenty-five years experience in planning consultancy.
