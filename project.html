Urban Planning
Da-Ya provide all types of expert advice on all planning and urban development matters. We offer a complete range of services and can assist at all stages of the development process. We don’t believe in a ‘one size fits all’ approach to planning and management applications. Each client and each site has specific requirements and we provide a tailored solution for each project. We work alongside a range of associated individual consultants, often leading large multi-disciplinary teams. Our focus is always on identifying the right expert for each specific project.
Asset Management Plan
Da-Ya also undertakes Asset inventory and prepares Asset Management Plan (AMP) for cities that is a plan developed for the management of one or more infrastructure asset classes with a view to operating, maintaining and renewing the assets within the class in the most cost effective manner possible, whilst providing a specific level of service.
Land Inventory
Da-Ya working on to know the land and land resources with basic attributes and then to have a well-organized geo-database along with modern land information management system, Which also involves registration of all parcels with owners and Unique Parcel Identification Code (UPIC) within the given built-up city boundary, linking the parcels with owners and other basic attributes (Non-spatial data), defining urban blocks with unique IDs and Land Use categorization at block level. The consultant has also recognized and reorganized all available archives (Files) at block and parcel levels and gave unique IDs based on well designed and simple system for land holding files management.

Environmental Impact
Major Urban environmental issues are air pollution, improper handling of solid and liquid waste, lack urban green space and sound pollution. Towns greatly contribute to climate change sharing nearly three per cent of Ethiopia's total polluted gas emissions. This is perhaps, due to twinned effects of fast urban population growth rate and lack of efficient climate resilient programme. Hence, Da-Ya is also working on this environmental strategic activity area includes Green Areas and Parks, Road Side and Median Greens, River Side Greens, Wetlands and Play Grounds.
